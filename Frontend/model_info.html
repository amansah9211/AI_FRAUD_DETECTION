RobustScaler Mathematical Transformation

StandardScaler uses mean and variance which are heavily distorted by high-value fraud transaction outliers. FraudGuard AI uses RobustScaler:

x_scaled = (x - Q2(x)) / (Q3(x) - Q1(x))

Where Q2(x) is the median feature value, and IQR = Q3(x) - Q1(x) is the interquartile range across non-fraud historical baselines.
